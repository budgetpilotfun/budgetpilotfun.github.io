Lease ending soon?

You usually have three choices: hand it back, trade it in, or buy it. Buying only makes sense if the payoff is fair against what the car is worth, so run those numbers first.

  • Your options at lease end
  • Should you buy out your lease?
  • When your leased car is worth more than the payoff
  • Twelve questions before a lease buyout

If keeping it comes out ahead and you need to finance the purchase, lease buyout financing is arranged by firms such as Champion Auto Finance. It is not a lender itself, so terms depend on lender underwriting.
